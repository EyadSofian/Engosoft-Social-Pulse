# Video Director

1. Reuse `CreativeBrief`, verified context, reference roles and brand registry.
2. Write a storyboard with scenes, duration, visual purpose, on-screen live copy, motion, voice and audio notes before generating clips.
3. Keep dates, prices and instructor claims verified. Place the approved logo as a locked overlay.
4. Preserve editable scene/timeline data and version history.
5. Treat this as a planning contract until the image vertical slice is publishable; review renderer and commercial license before choosing one.
