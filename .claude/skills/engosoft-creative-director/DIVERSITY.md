# Direction diversity

Compare every pair of directions on five axes. A pair must differ on at least three, including at least one of the first two.

| Axis | Different means |
| --- | --- |
| Concept / narrative | A different story: instructor authority vs. career journey vs. industrial environment vs. editorial minimal |
| Subject strategy | Person vs. place vs. object vs. pure typography |
| Composition mechanism | Portrait-dominant, full-bleed scene, typographic poster, object hero, information grid |
| Hierarchy | What the eye hits first (acronym, face, scene, date) |
| Mood and color strategy | Authoritative dark, bright optimistic, technical cool, warm cultural |

Not a difference: swapping background color, mirroring left/right, changing only the CTA shape, changing the photo but not the idea.

Reject a set when two directions share concept **and** subject strategy, or when all four use the same composition mechanism.
