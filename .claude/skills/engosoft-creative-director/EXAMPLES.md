## What the reference posts teach

The supplied Engosoft posts show recurring structures worth reusing conceptually: an instructor cut-out with a large course acronym, an information bar with date/time/platform, a credential stack under the name, and bright accent labels. They all carry the **legacy** logo; take structure and hierarchy from them, never logo treatment.
