## Reference DNA

Two to five sentences a designer would write. Example:

> A premium course promotion dominated by the instructor, who fills about 40% of the frame. A flat brand-blue field keeps attention on the face and the course acronym, which has the highest typographic priority. A bright accent marks only key facts. A lower information bar carries schedule metadata. It feels authoritative rather than playful.

DNA is what the compiler paraphrases into role instructions. A bare family label ("webinar") is not enough.
