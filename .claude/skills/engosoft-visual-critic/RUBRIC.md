# Rubric

| Axis | Look at | 9–10 means | ≤ 5 means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Design | Composition, hierarchy, balance, focus, whitespace, flow | A senior designer would sign it | Template-like, cluttered, or no clear focal point |
| Marketing | Message clarity, CTA, audience fit, campaign strength | Message understood in 2 seconds | Viewer cannot tell what is offered |
| Image quality | Anatomy, hands, faces, duplicated or malformed objects, perspective, realism, stray text, artifacts | No visible defect at 100% zoom | Any prominent defect |
| Brand | Engosoft fit, not generic Canva, not repetitive, room for the real logo | Recognizably Engosoft without a blue flood | Off-brand palette or no logo zone |
| Copy | Arabic and English rendering, factual match with canonical copy | All critical text exact | Any critical fact wrong or unreadable |
| Platform | Safe areas, readability at phone size, density, crop | Works at feed thumbnail size | Text in UI zones or too dense |
| Originality | Distinct from the other directions and recent posts | Clearly its own idea | Near-duplicate |

Calibration: store human 1–5 ratings next to critic scores in the eval runs and review disagreements before changing thresholds.
