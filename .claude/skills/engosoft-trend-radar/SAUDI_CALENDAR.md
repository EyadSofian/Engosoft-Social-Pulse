## Tone during Ramadan

- Post after Taraweeh (≈ 22:00–01:00 KSA); avoid iftar hour.
- Softer copy; "ابدأ رمضان بمهارة جديدة" works, aggressive urgency does not.
- Do not use Quranic verses or hadith in sales posts.
