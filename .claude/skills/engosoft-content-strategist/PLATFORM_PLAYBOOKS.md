# Platform playbooks (checked October 2026)

Numbers here are mirrored in `src/domain/platform-content.ts` (`PLATFORM_SPECS`). Where public sources disagree, we pick the safe side and say so.

## Saudi audience snapshot

- **Snapchat** is the Kingdom's daily habit: ~26M monthly users, >90% of 13–34 year-olds (Snap figures). Vertical, raw, face-to-camera, Saudi dialect.
- **X** remains the news/opinion channel; **Instagram** ~20–22M; **LinkedIn** ~11M, mostly 25–34 — our core professional buyer; **TikTok** strong for discovery and search.
- Week runs **Sunday–Thursday**; weekend Friday–Saturday.

## Instagram (feed, carousel, Reels)

- Caption limit 2,200; the first ~125 characters show before "more" → hook there.
- **Hashtags: 3–5.** Instagram announced a 5-hashtag cap (Dec 2025); some accounts still see 30. Writing 5 or fewer is safe everywhere.
- Links are not clickable → "الرابط في البايو".
- Reels rank on watch time, replays and **sends per reach** (DM shares). Write for "send this to a colleague".
- Keywords in caption + on-screen text feed Instagram search.
- Carousels: 6–10 slides, one idea per slide, slide 1 = hook, last slide = CTA + save prompt.

## TikTok

- In-app caption up to 4,000 characters, but API/scheduler posting is 2,200 → we cap at 2,200.
- Caption works as search text: course name + problem in plain words, 3–5 hashtags (one broad, two niche).
- Hook in the first 1–2 seconds, on screen AND spoken. 20–45 s for course promos.

## Snapchat (Spotlight / Stories)

- Caption ≤160 characters, 1–3 hashtags, no links.
- Stories: 3–6 frames, each one sentence, face/hands on screen, Saudi dialect, poll/question sticker idea on frame 2–3.

## LinkedIn

- Fold at ~140 characters; dwell time is the main signal → short paragraphs separated by blank lines, insight first.
- 0–3 hashtags, at the end (keywords in the body matter more than hashtags now).
- Document/carousel posts: 5–10 pages, one idea each. Do not tag more than a few people.
- MSA, professional; at most one emoji.

## Facebook

- Fold at ~140 characters; up to 3 hashtags; link clickable on its own line.
- Older and Egyptian audiences are strong here; Egyptian or MSA depending on the post's market.

## YouTube (Shorts + descriptions)

- First ~150 characters of the description carry the keywords and benefit.
- Shorts title ≤ 100 characters, keyword first. 3 hashtags at the end.

## WhatsApp (status / broadcast / channel)

- 2–3 lines + link, no hashtags. Personal tone.

## Website

- 2–4 sentence news paragraph, keyword-aware, link at the end, no hashtags or emojis.
