Privacy Notice

Engosoft Mobde3 is an internal social media planning and analytics dashboard operated by Engosoft.

Information used from TikTok

When an authorized account is connected, Mobde3 uses TikTok Login Kit and the permissions approved by that account. For video reporting, it receives the account's TikTok identifier and public videos, including video IDs, captions or titles, publish times, share links, cover images, views, likes, comments, and shares. It does not publish, edit, or delete TikTok content.

How information is used and stored

The information is used by Engosoft's authorized social media team to review content performance. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted on the server using AES-256-GCM. The dashboard stores the reporting records in its Railway-hosted application database. Access to that database is limited to the application service.

Sharing and retention

Engosoft does not sell this TikTok data or use it for advertising. The data remains in the dashboard until an authorized administrator removes it. Revoking Mobde3 access in TikTok stops future API access; to request removal of stored dashboard data, contact info@engosoft.com.

Dashboard visibility

Mobde3 requires sign-in. Accounts are created by an Engosoft administrator for authorized team members, and only signed-in users can view the dashboard and its stored post reporting, including connected TikTok video details and metrics. Connect only company accounts that Engosoft is authorized to monitor.

Your choices

You can revoke the app's access from your TikTok account's app permissions at any time. TikTok's own privacy practices are described in its Privacy Policy.

For privacy questions about Mobde3, contact info@engosoft.com.
